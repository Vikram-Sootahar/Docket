National Database & Registration Authority (NADRA)
Application for CNIC Renewal

To renew your Computerized National Identity Card, please submit the following documents at your nearest NADRA office:

1. Original expired/expiring CNIC
2. Two recent passport-size photographs
3. Proof of current residential address (utility bill not older than 3 months)
4. Filled application Form-B (available at the counter or downloadable from nadra.gov.pk)

Processing Fee: PKR 750 (normal processing, 15 working days) or PKR 1,500 (urgent processing, 3 working days)

Applications are accepted Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. No prior appointment is required.
